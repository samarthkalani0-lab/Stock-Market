    How to read these charts

    Three ideas are enough to read everything on this page.

    The price

    The cyan line is what one share closed at each day. On its own it jumps around a lot, which makes the direction hard to see.

    The 50-day average

    The amber line averages the last 50 closing prices. It moves slowly, so it shows the trend. If it's sloping up, the stock has been climbing for weeks.

    Above or below

    A price above its average usually means buyers are in control right now. Below it usually means the opposite. It describes the past and doesn't predict the future.
